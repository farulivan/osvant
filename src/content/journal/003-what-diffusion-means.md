projection is how far a fragrance travels. diffusion is how it occupies
the space it travels through. most people mean the first when they say
the second, and the confusion sells a great deal of very loud perfume.

halo is built almost entirely around the difference.

> worn close it is almost nothing. at conversational distance it is
> unmistakable.

the trick is in the base. cashmeran and pale musk sit low and wide rather
than high and sharp, and the iris above them is pitched to spread instead
of project. the composition fills a room at the volume of a held breath.

this is harder to sell than a fragrance that announces itself from the
lift. it is also the reason people who wear halo tend to keep wearing it
long after the bottle that shouted at them has gone to the back of the
shelf.

concentration is not the same as presence. voltage is not the same as
noise.
