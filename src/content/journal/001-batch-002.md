batch 002 is fever, and it is five hundred bottles. not a limited edition
in the marketing sense — a limited edition in the sense that the saffron
lot behind it was five kilos and there is no second lot at that grade.

we said at the start that we would not reformulate to hit a price. this
is what that promise costs: when the material runs out, the batch closes.

> the alternative was a version of fever that was almost fever. we have
> smelled that compromise on other people's shelves.

what you get instead is a number on the base of the bottle and the
knowledge that the next one will be different. that is not scarcity as a
tactic. it is just what happens when you buy the good lot and refuse to
stretch it.

pre-orders open when the maceration finishes. six weeks, no earlier.
