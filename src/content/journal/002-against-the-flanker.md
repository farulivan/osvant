a flanker is what happens when a fragrance sells well and the house wants
to sell it twice. same name, one material swapped, _intense_ or _noir_ or
_elixir_ on the box. the industry produces thousands a year.

we will not make one. not out of principle — out of arithmetic.

> five formulas is not a constraint we are working around. it is the
> product.

every flanker is a bet that the original was not quite right. we would
rather spend that year making the original right the first time, and then
leave it alone. halo took more revisions than the other four combined and
it still ships as one concentration, because a second concentration would
have been an admission that we stopped too early.

the collection is five. it was five at launch and it will be five in ten
years. if we ever release a sixth, it will be because something exists
that none of the five could do — not because the fourth one sold.
